Team Recap

AI · Sat, Nov 7

Team Xavier KTCS is 0-2-4 through six games, with 84 points scored and 119 allowed, and it is still searching for its first win. Giovani Pelaez has been a spark, with pick-six touchdowns and a standout over-the-defender catch, while captain Xavier Davenport has been a ball-hawk on defense with multiple picks, including a pick-six in the Halloween Bowl. Charlie Waddell has thrown seven touchdown passes, and Luther Nuesken caught three touchdowns in the Halloween Bowl while Finn Hopkins has thrown four TD passes. The team has dropped its last two games, a 70-42 Halloween Bowl loss and a 14-7 defeat to the Pineapples on November 5, where Davenport scored a touchdown. The next challenge is a rematch with the Pineapples on November 9, and taking better care of the football, which has been Waddell's biggest challenge with five interceptions, could help this group turn its close games into wins.

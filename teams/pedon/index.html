Team Recap

AI · Fri, Oct 30

The Team Pedon Pineapples are 1-0-4 and riding a W1 streak after a rollicking 70-42 Halloween Bowl playoff win over KTCS, their lone victory so far. Four regular-season games ended in ties, including three 7-7 finishes and a 14-14 draw, so the team has been hard to separate from its opponents. Maddox Marsaglia lit up the playoff game with three touchdowns and some eye-popping mossing, while Kayson Link kept finding receivers in the end zone. Braxton Iliff-Murphy and Carson Tabaka each added two playoff touchdowns, Liam Bair recorded two sacks, and Maddox Pedon made a notable goal-line catch that was ruled no touchdown. Next up is a regular-season rematch with KTCS on November 2, and the Pineapples will look to turn their ties into a win.

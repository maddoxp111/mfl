🃏Lineup

Set your Lineup

Set a Lineup of 11 player cards. Every MFL game, each card earns coins for what its player does in the box score. Every fantasy point pays 100 coins. The Lineup saved when Pick'em locks (4 minutes before kickoff) is the one that counts, and it carries over until you change it.

Better cards earn moreUp to 5×

Good plays are multiplied by the card: Base 1× Silver 1.25× Gold 1.5× Legend 2× Mythic 3× Dark Matter 4× Zenith 5×. Specialist cards earn 3× more on their specialty. Mistakes are never multiplied. Example: a TD pass on a Legend card is 3,500 × 2 = 7,000 coins. Some fun cards add a bonus every game they play: Benchwarmer +2,000, Participation Trophy +1,500, Rubber Ducky +7,500.

💸Where coins go

  • Picks that miss (at least 10 coins each).
  • Packs: 1,000 to 650,000 coins.
  • Risk mode and Double or Double games you lose. Double or Double can take you below zero.
  • Head to Head games you lose: your wager.
  • Lineup mistakes: a card loses 2,500 when its player throws an interception.
  • Your player: you throw an interception −25,000, a penalty on you −25,000.
  • Voided games: if the commissioner voids a game, anything a pick on it paid is taken back.

Coins are play money. They can't be bought, sold or traded for anything real. How Pick'em works

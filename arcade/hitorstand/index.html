🃏 Hit or Stand

Beat the dealer to 21. Play up to 150,000 a hand.

Dealer
21 in two cards pays 3 to 2 · Dealer stands on all 17s

Log in to play for coins at the table.

Log in Sign up free

How it works

  • Bet 100 to 150,000 coins a hand (never more than you have). Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.
  • A win pays 1 to 1. A 21 in two cards (an ace and a ten card) pays 3 to 2: 150,000 wins 225,000. A tie is a push: your bet comes back.
  • Double on any first two cards: your bet doubles and you get one more card. Split a pair once into two hands, each with its own bet; split aces get one card each, and 21 after a split counts as a plain 21. Doubling and splitting need the coins to cover the extra bet.
  • The dealer stands on all 17s and checks for 21 when showing an ace or a ten. 6 decks, shuffled fresh for every hand. No insurance.
  • Leave in the middle of a hand and nothing is lost: it's still here when you come back, and it's played out as a stand when you deal the next hand (or come back after 30 minutes).
